## Executive summary

Belgian buyers search in **Dutch/Flemish** (`guest post kopen`, `backlinks kopen`, `linkbuilding`, `gesponsord artikel`), **Belgian French** (`acheter guest post`, `netlinking`, `publirédactionnel`), and English loanwords (`guest post Belgium`). Competitive SERPs: **WhitePress**, NL/FR marketplaces, local netlinking bureaus. Wedge: one `/be/` locale (not a second NL or FR site), `.be` country filter, EUR wallet, London HQ (Topurlz Ltd) — **no invented Belgian ondernemingsnummer/BTW**. Do not steal unprefixed NL `marktplaats` / `guest-post-kopen` or FR `acheter-*` unique slugs; Belgium conversion URLs stay unique (`/be/koop-guest-post-belgie`, `/be/gesponsord-artikel`). Skip city/competitor doorways (`/be/brussel`, `/be/whitepress`).

SEOLinkBuildings’ wedge: **self-serve marketplace** + **verified publishers** + **EUR wallet** + **Europe catalog** + clear order-to-live-URL tracking.

**How to use the metrics below**
- **Traffic potential (0–100):** relative demand × intent strength (not monthly visits).
- **Difficulty (0–100) / band:** SERP hardness heuristic from intent + known local competitors.
- **Competition density:** how crowded the SERP feels (Low → Very High).
- **Keyword density target:** on-page guidance for the primary term (not measured density).
- **Opportunity score (0–100):** balances traffic potential, inverse difficulty, and business fit.
- **Priority rank:** 1 = strongest opportunity among the 103 (easier wins rise; core money terms may rank lower if SERP is Very Hard — still ship as P0 landings).

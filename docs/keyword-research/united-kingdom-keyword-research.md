## Executive summary

UK buyers search English money terms (`buy guest posts uk`, `guest posts uk`, `buy backlinks uk`, `.co.uk guest posts`). Competitive SERPs: **FATJOE** (UK), Digitaloft, Adsy, Collaborator, iCopify. Default English **is** UK: unprefixed `/` and `/marketplace` stay British / `.uk` / `.co.uk`. Do **not** canonicalize UK pages to `/ie/` or treat Ireland as a UK dump. Ireland uses `/uk/…-ireland` slugs on this locale. HQ is Topurlz Ltd, London — that is a fact, not an invented local office.

SEOLinkBuildings’ wedge: **self-serve marketplace** + **verified publishers** + **EUR wallet** + **Europe catalog** + clear order-to-live-URL tracking.

**How to use the metrics below**
- **Traffic potential (0–100):** relative demand × intent strength (not monthly visits).
- **Difficulty (0–100) / band:** SERP hardness heuristic from intent + known local competitors.
- **Competition density:** how crowded the SERP feels (Low → Very High).
- **Keyword density target:** on-page guidance for the primary term (not measured density).
- **Opportunity score (0–100):** balances traffic potential, inverse difficulty, and business fit.
- **Priority rank:** 1 = strongest opportunity among the 99 (easier wins rise; core money terms may rank lower if SERP is Very Hard — still ship as P0 landings).

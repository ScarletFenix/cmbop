## Executive summary

US buyers search English money terms (`buy guest posts`, `buy backlinks`, `guest post marketplace`, `niche edits`, `white label link building`). Competitive SERPs: **FatJoe**, **Adsy**, **iCopify**, **Collaborator**, **INSERT.LINK**, **The HOTH**, **Loganix**. Digital PR adjacent: Connectively/HARO. Wedge: self-serve marketplace + verified publishers + EUR wallet + Europe/.com catalog. **No invented US HQ/EIN.** Public US chrome is `/us/` (en-US); default unprefixed English stays UK (en-GB).

SEOLinkBuildings’ wedge: **self-serve marketplace** + **verified publishers** + **EUR wallet** + **Europe catalog** + clear order-to-live-URL tracking.

**How to use the metrics below**
- **Traffic potential (0–100):** relative demand × intent strength (not monthly visits).
- **Difficulty (0–100) / band:** SERP hardness heuristic from intent + known local competitors.
- **Competition density:** how crowded the SERP feels (Low → Very High).
- **Keyword density target:** on-page guidance for the primary term (not measured density).
- **Opportunity score (0–100):** balances traffic potential, inverse difficulty, and business fit.
- **Priority rank:** 1 = strongest opportunity among the 90 (easier wins rise; core money terms may rank lower if SERP is Very Hard — still ship as P0 landings).

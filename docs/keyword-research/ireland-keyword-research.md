## Executive summary

Irish buyers search `buy guest posts ireland`, `guest posts ireland`, `.ie backlinks`, `irish media guest posts`. Ireland is **not** a second UK homepage: focus Ireland / Irish / `.ie` / Dublin; generic UK pages stay UK / British / `.uk` / `.co.uk`. Architecture: dedicated pages under `/uk/` with `-ireland` slugs (`/uk/marketplace-ireland/`, `/uk/buy-guest-posts-ireland/`). `/uk` itself 301s to `/`. Do not canonicalize Ireland pages to `/ie/`. Hreflang is **en-GB** (self + x-default), not `en-IE`. Catalog country code `ie`. **No invented Irish office or VAT**; HQ remains Topurlz Ltd, London. Checkout is EUR.

SEOLinkBuildings’ wedge: **self-serve marketplace** + **verified publishers** + **EUR wallet** + **Europe catalog** + clear order-to-live-URL tracking.

**How to use the metrics below**
- **Traffic potential (0–100):** relative demand × intent strength (not monthly visits).
- **Difficulty (0–100) / band:** SERP hardness heuristic from intent + known local competitors.
- **Competition density:** how crowded the SERP feels (Low → Very High).
- **Keyword density target:** on-page guidance for the primary term (not measured density).
- **Opportunity score (0–100):** balances traffic potential, inverse difficulty, and business fit.
- **Priority rank:** 1 = strongest opportunity among the 83 (easier wins rise; core money terms may rank lower if SERP is Very Hard — still ship as P0 landings).
